Welcome

Welcome to my website. This site introduces who I am as a student, what I study, and the academic and professional goals I hope to pursue in the future. As a psychology major, I am especially interested in children's language, narrative, and memory development.

Why Psychology?

"Psychology is the science of immediate experience." — Wilhelm Wundt

Psychology, as defined by American Psychological Association, is the study of human mind and behavior. It's not the trick that people use to do mind reading, unfortunately. I'm interested in psychology because it is a fun process to learn about what people think and how they behvae, and more importantly, why the do so. I'm especially interested in children and adolescents because childhood experiences are important. Thus I want to understand them more and help them as much as I can.

What Do I Study?

I basically study anything you think that is relevant to human beings, including cognition, emotion, development, sexuality, language. I also take course which are more application oriented, such as abnormal psychology (mental health issues), and legal pscyhology.

Academic Interests
