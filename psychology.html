What is psychology?

As defined by the American Psychological Association (APA), psychology is the understanding of human mind and behavior. In other words, psychology cares about all human expereinces from human brain to expereince and from embryo to aged population.

Language

Language is a stuctured system that people use to communicate with others. It has three key components: productivity, symbolism, and displacement. Language is not equal to communication because communication sometimes doesn't require a "system". For example, dogs can bark, but is that language? The answer might be NO, because their communication don't contain the displacement component of language. It is also so interesting to see language has so many variations!

Narrative

How you tell a story? This a process of narration. It requires may cognitive abilities, such as executive function. For example, you need to organize your words and timeline so that you can tell a clear story. The other part of narrative is why do you tell a story? Or, what purposes do you have? With different purposes (e.g., persuaion, entertainment), we can tell the same story in very different ways. How will this impact our real life?
